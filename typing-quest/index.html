How to play
  • ⌨️ Type the letters you see inside the clouds. The letter you need next is underlined and lights up on the helper keyboard.
  • 🌈 Each key is colored by which finger should press it. Keep your pointer fingers on the bumpy F and J keys and reach from there.
  • 🌤️ Calm Practice: one word at a time, no rush, no hearts. Perfect for learning a new row.
  • ⛈️ Sky Adventure: words drift toward Isa. Finish a word and Isa blasts it with fire! If a word reaches her, she loses a heart. Lose three and she needs a rest.
  • 🔥 Type a whole word with no mistakes to build a streak for bonus points.
  • ⭐ Earn up to three stars on every level. Try to get all of them!
  • ⏸ Press Esc to pause.
